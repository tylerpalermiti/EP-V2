About EP Advisory

A financial team, without the overhead of building one.

EP Advisory was founded to give business owners access to the financial expertise they need without the cost and complexity of building a full internal accounting department.

Most business owners are good at the work itself — not accounting. As the business grows, the financial side gets more complicated. EP Advisory exists to fill that gap: bookkeeping, financial reporting, and advisory support that scales with the business, delivered by one accountable team.

Built from the inside of accounting

A firm shaped by what actually breaks down as businesses grow.

Our team's experience is CPA-level and GAAP-grounded — built around outsourced accounting, financial reporting, and business advisory for small and midsize businesses, including medical practices, construction and trade businesses, and growing companies. Not general practice, not tax preparation, not audit work. Just the discipline of getting a business's financial picture right, then making it useful.

You shouldn't need to build an entire internal accounting department to have access to good financial leadership.
