About EP Advisory

A financial team, without the overhead of building one.

EP Advisory was founded to give business owners access to the financial expertise they need without the cost and complexity of building a full internal accounting department.

Most business owners are good at the work itself — not accounting. As the business grows, the financial side gets more complicated. EP Advisory exists to fill that gap: bookkeeping, financial reporting, and advisory support that scales with the business, delivered by one accountable team.

Built from the inside of accounting

A firm shaped by what actually breaks down as businesses grow.

Our team's experience is CPA-level and GAAP-grounded — built around outsourced accounting, financial reporting, and business advisory for small and midsize businesses, including medical practices, construction and trade businesses, and growing companies. Not general practice, not tax preparation, not audit work. Just the discipline of getting a business's financial picture right, then making it useful.

You shouldn't need to build an entire internal accounting department to have access to good financial leadership.

Anthony Palermiti is a founder of EP Advisory and leads the firm's fraud, forensic, and internal-controls practice. At EP Advisory, Anthony brings an investigative and risk-focused mindset to financial analysis, fraud awareness, internal-control assessments, and the future development of the firm's fraud-examination services. He is committed to helping business owners strengthen financial processes, identify potential risk, and protect what they have worked to build.

Anthony is also a husband and father of two boys. He is building EP Advisory around the values of service, integrity, disciplined work, and clear decision-making.

Tyler Palermiti, CPA

Partner
CPA Former College Quarterback

Tyler is a CPA and former college quarterback who built his career in public accounting before helping start EP Advisory. He's focused on helping business owners understand what their numbers mean for where the business is headed.

I'm Tyler Palermiti, a CPA, entrepreneur, former college quarterback, and Partner at EP Advisory.

My path to accounting started with a strong foundation in both business and athletics. I earned my degree from McDaniel College, where I played quarterback for the football team. Being a college athlete taught me discipline, leadership, accountability, and the importance of working toward a common goal. Those lessons continue to influence the way I approach business and relationships today.

After college, I built my career in public accounting, gaining experience in outsourced accounting, financial reporting, client advisory, audits, and working directly with business owners. I went on to become a Certified Public Accountant and developed a particular interest in helping businesses understand not just their financial statements, but what those numbers actually mean for the future of their business.

That experience eventually led me to help build EP Advisory. I wanted to create a firm that felt different from the traditional accounting experience — more personal, more accessible, and more involved.

Outside of work, I'm someone who enjoys staying active, following sports, traveling, meeting new people, and having a good time with friends and family. I take my work seriously, but I don't believe business has to be stuffy or overly formal.

At the end of the day, I'm an entrepreneur who happens to be a CPA. I enjoy building relationships, solving problems, competing, and helping people build something they're proud of.

"Work hard. Have fun. Build something worth being proud of."

Charles Eggleston, MBA
