About EP Advisory

A financial team, without the overhead of building one.

EP Advisory was founded to give business owners access to the financial expertise they need without the cost and complexity of building a full internal accounting department.

Most business owners started their company because they're good at the work itself — construction, professional services, client work, operations. Not accounting. As the business grows, the financial side gets more complicated: a bookkeeper who records transactions but doesn't explain them, financial statements that don't quite make sense, cash-flow uncertainty, no real forecasting, no consistent month-end process, no one helping interpret profitability. Eventually, the business needs more than bookkeeping. It needs financial clarity.

EP Advisory exists to fill that gap — bookkeeping, financial reporting, and advisory support that scales with the business, delivered by one accountable team instead of a rotating cast of contractors.

Built from the inside of accounting

A firm shaped by what actually breaks down as businesses grow.

EP Advisory was built from years of experience working inside accounting — seeing firsthand where business owners struggle with their financial information, where accounting processes break down as companies grow, and how much more useful financial reporting can become when someone takes the time to understand the business behind the numbers.

That experience is CPA-level and GAAP-grounded, built specifically around outsourced accounting for small and midsize businesses — not general practice, not tax preparation, not audit work. Just the discipline of getting a business's financial picture right, then making it useful.

You shouldn't need to build an entire internal accounting department to have access to good financial leadership.

A note on how we work

Confident, straightforward, and honest about what we do — and don't — do.

EP Advisory is a bookkeeping, financial reporting, and business advisory firm. We don't prepare tax returns or perform audits — when those are needed, we're glad to coordinate with a tax professional you trust. What we do, we do thoroughly: accurate books, reporting you can understand, and advisory support grounded in your actual numbers.
