Common questions

Everything you're probably wondering.

How is this different from a bookkeeper I'd hire directly?+

You get a dedicated bookkeeper backed by a full team — so there's no single point of failure if someone's out, and the work is reviewed before it reaches you. As you grow, you can add reporting or controller-level oversight without starting over with someone new.

Do I need to switch accounting software?+

Usually not. We work within the systems you already use, and if your current setup is holding you back, we'll recommend a change and handle the migration for you.

What does onboarding actually involve?+

After the discovery call, we reconcile your historical books, clean up the chart of accounts, and get everything current — typically within two weeks, depending on how far behind things are.

Can I start with just bookkeeping and add more later?+

Yes — that's how most engagements start. Financial reporting and controller services layer on top whenever you're ready, with the same team and no re-onboarding.

Do you handle tax filing?+

We don't file taxes directly, but we keep your books clean and organized so your CPA can file quickly and accurately — and we're glad to coordinate with them directly.
