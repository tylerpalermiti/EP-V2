EP Advisory was built around a simple idea: too many growing businesses have access to bookkeeping, but not enough financial insight. Somewhere between recording transactions and truly understanding a business, most firms stop. EP Advisory was created to bridge that gap.

The firm is built from years of experience working inside accounting — seeing firsthand where business owners struggle with their financial information, where accounting processes break down as companies grow, and how much more useful financial reporting becomes when someone takes the time to understand the business behind the numbers.

You shouldn't need to build an entire internal accounting department to have access to good financial leadership.
